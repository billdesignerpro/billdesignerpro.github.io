Features

Small-business billing, made more professional

A demo of a customizable Android invoice app. Final features depend on the scope agreed for your business.

🎨

Business-specific branding

Customize shop name, logo, business details and relevant authorized-partner information.

📄

A4 invoice PDF

Create a clean, print-friendly invoice layout with item lines, totals and tax details where configured.

🧮

Tax-aware calculations

Support the agreed tax presentation and calculations for your invoice workflow. Tax configuration should be reviewed for your business before use.

🗃️

Invoice history

Save invoice records locally and reopen past invoices from the app.

🔎

Search when you need it

Find past invoices using supported search fields, including customer name, phone number and invoice number.

📤

Share PDFs easily

Send invoice PDFs through WhatsApp, email or other supported sharing options.

✉️

Email options

Where configured, email an invoice to the customer and send a backup copy to a shop-controlled email address.

💳

Payment details

Configure the payment methods and cheque details needed for your workflow, as agreed in the project scope.

☁️

Google Drive app-data sync

Optional sync can keep supported invoice data aligned across devices signed in with the same Google account. It uses the app's private Drive app-data area; the current implementation does not upload invoice PDFs.

🔢

Invoice numbering

Discuss invoice numbering and automatic next-number behavior as part of your customization requirements.

🛠️

Shop-specific configuration

Set agreed shop details and branding without making the app look like a generic template.

📱

Android-first experience

Designed for everyday use on Android phones. Device compatibility and final requirements are confirmed before delivery.

Features are agreed before development

The demo helps you understand the concept. Your final app is tailored to the business details and features agreed in your quotation.
